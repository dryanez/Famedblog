Language preparation is one part of a medical career in Germany. Recognition of qualifications, permission to practise and immigration requirements depend on your individual route and the responsible authority.

## Ask about your circumstances

Our career page links to Veldener HR Referenten (VHR). Contact the provider directly for current job-placement, accommodation, course availability and any eligibility or cost conditions. Confirm the exact services and terms before making a commitment.

[Contact VHR](https://www.vhr-referenten.de/kontakt)

A language course, FaMED result or preparation purchase does not itself grant Approbation, Berufserlaubnis, a visa or a job.

[Read how to check recognition requirements](/blog/famed-anerkennung-bundeslaender-2026), or [learn about the FaMED exam](/what-is-famed).
