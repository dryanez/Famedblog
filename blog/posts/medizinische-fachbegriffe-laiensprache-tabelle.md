## 6. Practical Exam Tip: The "Echo-Translation" Technique

When interacting with patients in the FaMED station, use the **Echo-Translation Technique**:
If you accidentally utter a technical term, immediately self-correct with lay phrasing:

> *"Wir werden die Laborwerte auf eine Hyperglykämie kontrollieren — das bedeutet, wir schauen, ob Ihr Blutzuckerspiegel erhöht ist."*

This demonstrates to examiners that you are conscious of communication barriers and actively safeguard patient clarity.
