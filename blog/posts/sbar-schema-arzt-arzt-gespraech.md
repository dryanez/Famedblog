The doctor–doctor station contains **two patient presentations**. Its published duration is **23 minutes including reading**; the current practice test allows **at most 11 minutes per situation**. The cases need to be kept separate.

You communicate relevant supplied information in professional German and answer follow-up questions. This is a language task, not a requirement to recall a treatment algorithm from memory.

## SBAR is an organising tool

SBAR can give a practice presentation a clear route. It is not the official mandatory answer template or a numerical scoring rubric.

| Part | Useful question |
|---|---|
| Situation | Who is being presented, and why? |
| Background | Which reported symptoms and history matter? |
| Assessment | Which findings and interpretation are actually supplied? |
| Recommendation | What course, plan or requested next step belongs to this task? |

A supplied case can call for a different emphasis. Do not force information into an empty category.

## A short fictional source-to-speech exercise

Practice notes: Frau Beispiel, 54; cough for five days; no temperature value supplied; medication list available in the task; examination report not supplied.

A faithful opening might be:

> Ich stelle Frau Beispiel, eine 54-jährige Patientin, vor. Sie berichte über seit fünf Tagen bestehenden Husten. Eine gemessene Temperatur ist in den vorliegenden Angaben nicht dokumentiert.

This is deliberately a short language exercise. It does not support adding normal vital signs, prescribing medication or announcing a diagnosis.

## Respond to a follow-up precisely

If asked about information that is present, answer briefly and accurately. If it is missing, say so:

> Dazu liegen mir in der Aufgabeninformation keine Angaben vor.

Distinguish „bereits durchgeführt“, „geplant“ and „von mir vorgeschlagen“. Never turn a suggestion into an intervention supposedly already performed. Clinical knowledge is not separately assessed in FaMed, but your wording must not distort the information.

## Practise two cases independently

Make separate notes for each practice case. After speaking, compare your recording with the supplied source: did you mix identities, times, findings or plans? Then repeat only the unclear section. Use the [differential-diagnosis phrasing exercise](/blog/differentialdiagnose-formulieren-deutsch) to practise degrees of certainty.

The [written station](/blog/famed-arztbrief-soap-schema) has a different task: anamnesis from its video in complete sentences. Do not carry this presentation's facts into it.
