## Start with your actual needs

Try a short exercise in history taking, informed consent, case presentation and written anamnesis. Identify where you lose information or clarity. Reading alone does not establish how you communicate under the task instructions.

## What the guide helps you organise

The guide gives an adaptable eight-week schedule covering speaking and writing, with review points and links to useful preparation resources. It is a suggested study resource, not an official timetable or a passing guarantee.

## Adapt rather than rush

Choose a sustainable session length, include writing from the beginning and leave time to review before repeating. Use the provider’s current task instructions when adding timed practice. Your language level, available time and feedback determine how you should adapt the schedule.
