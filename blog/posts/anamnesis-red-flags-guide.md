When a person describes a concerning symptom, clear language matters. In this article we practise how to clarify that description and report what was said. It is not a complete diagnostic checklist, and individual symptom questions cannot rule out an emergency.

For FaMed, the relevant skill is faithful professional communication. Medical knowledge is not separately assessed. There is no verified universal „red flag missed = automatic fail“ rule in this review.

## Turn a vague phrase into an answerable question

A person may say „Ich bekomme schlecht Luft“, „Mir wird schwarz vor Augen“ or „Das Bein ist pelzig“. Do not replace these immediately with a diagnosis. Ask about what the person means:

> Wenn Sie sagen, das Bein sei pelzig: Fühlt es sich taub an, kribbelt es oder meinen Sie etwas anderes?

Then clarify when it began, how it changed and what else the person noticed. Our [patient-idiom article](/blog/deutsche-patientensprache-redewendungen) gives further examples.

## Separate information groups

| Group | Example question |
|---|---|
| Beginning | „Seit wann bemerken Sie das?“ |
| Change | „Was hat sich seitdem verändert?“ |
| Location | „Wo genau spüren Sie die Beschwerden?“ |
| Associated symptoms | „Welche weiteren Beschwerden sind hinzugekommen?“ |
| Context | „Was haben Sie unmittelbar davor gemacht?“ |

Select relevant follow-ups rather than applying every question in every scenario. For pain, [OPQRST](/blog/pain-anamnesis-guide) can support your memory.

## Do not turn a symptom list into a diagnosis

> Der Patient berichte über plötzlich aufgetretene Beschwerden. Zum weiteren Verlauf liegen in den vorliegenden Angaben noch keine Informationen vor.

Use the actual supplied clinical findings or plan if the assignment includes them. Do not add an examination you have not performed or a negative result that was never stated.

## Review the recording

After a fictional practice interview, check three things: did you understand the person's own words, did you clarify the important uncertainty, and did your summary add or change any facts?
